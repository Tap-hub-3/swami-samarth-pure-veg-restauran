About Our Restaurant

Shri Swami Samarth Family Restaurant & Fast Food Center is a pure vegetarian restaurant where families sit together and enjoy a good meal in a clean, calm setting.

From Maharashtrian favourites like misal pav and thali to South Indian dosas, Punjabi sabzis, Chinese and quick fast food, our kitchen cooks every dish fresh and keeps the menu simple and honest.
